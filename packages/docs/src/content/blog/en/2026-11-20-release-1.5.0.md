Hi everyone! Gram `v1.5.0` is now available.

This release goes back to basics and focuses on writing `.gram` files themselves, making the syntax both more natural and better suited to how real recipes are written. In a real recipe, you add measures together (`1 cup + 1 tbsp`), dose eggs per `100 g` of flour, bake for `1h30`, write `1,5 kg` with a decimal comma, watch the browning, and note `(fan)` next to `160°C`. Gram `v1.5.0` brings all of this straight into the language, without breaking existing recipes and without ever guessing when an expression is ambiguous.

*(To update your setup, run `gram upgrade` in your terminal, or `npm install -g @gram-lang/cli` if you are new to Gram.)*

### Deprecations

- **`renderer.runSheetRange` in `@gram-lang/i18n`**: deprecated since `1.5.0`, will be removed in `2.0.0`. The run sheet and step timers now both format duration ranges through `formatDurationRange()` from `@gram-lang/renderer` (`8–16 h` instead of `8 h to 16 h`). See [Deprecated features](/docs/how-to/deprecations/#the-runsheetrange-string).

### What's new in `.gram` syntax

Here is a quick look at what a recipe can now express naturally:

```gram
[Mix] Whisk @flour{1 cup + 1 tbsp} with @eggs{1 / 100 g @&flour} and @milk{0,25 l}.

[Bake] At ^{160°C}(fan) for ~{1h30}, until ={golden}(a skewer comes out clean).
```

#### Measuring and proportioning

- **Compound quantities**: ingredients, composite parents (`<@oranges{1 lb + 4 oz}`), and intermediates (`&dough{1 kg + 200 g}`) now accept any chain of `+` and `-` terms, including fractions and ranges (`@flour{1/2 cup + 1-2 tbsp}`, `@sugar{1 cup - 2 tbsp}`, `@butter{1 cup + 20 g}`). It brings everyday kitchen phrasing—combining two measures, separating two additions, or mixing weight and spoons—straight into the syntax. Steps keep the author's exact wording, while ambiguous cases (missing units, or mixing a percentage with a sum) are rejected rather than guessed.
- **Decimal commas**: `{1,5 kg}` and `{1.5 kg}` now mean the same thing across every braced number (ingredients, percentages, temperatures, timers, and section anchors). Because Gram has no thousands separator, a comma followed by three digits (`{1,000 g}`) is still read as a decimal (`1 g`) and flagged with a warning in case a thousand was meant.
- **Unit ratios**: express quantities relative to another ingredient across units or piece counts—`@eggs{1 / 100 g @&flour}`, `@salt{15 g / 1 L @&water}`, or homemade preparations like `&stock{250 ml / 100 g @&rice}` (percentages on homemade preparations like `&stock{250% @&rice}` now work too). Compatible units resolve at compile time; cross-family ratios (mass, volume, counts) use the ingredient database.

#### Natural durations and proportional timers

- **Natural timers and anchors**: write durations the way recipes do, in English or French, with or without spaces—`~{1h30}`, `~{1 hr 30 mins}`, `~{1 heure 30}`, `~{1h + 30min}`, `~{1:30 h}`, `~{5'30"}`, `~{45 min - 1 h}`, and section anchors like `~{-1h30}` or `~{-1j 12h}`. Genuinely ambiguous forms like `~{1:30}` (hours or minutes?) raise a warning with a suggested rewrite.
- **Proportional timers**: scale a cooking time by weight or piece count with `~{15 min / 500 g @&roast}` or `~{4 min / @&cutlet}`.

#### Cooking cues and temperatures

- **Target states**: mark inline checkpoints anywhere in a step, with an optional glued verification note: `Whisk until ={ribbon stage}(the batter falls back in a continuous ribbon).` Target states render across all views without affecting scaling, scheduling, or shopping lists.
- **Temperature notes**: attach a parenthetical note directly to a temperature's closing brace for oven mode or probe placement—`^{160°C}(fan)`, `^oven{180°C}(grill)`, `^{63°C}(internal)`. Spaced parentheses remain plain step text.

### Engine, planning, and tools

The rest of the pipeline follows these syntax additions seamlessly:

- **Scaling and shopping lists**: scaling multiplies every term in a sum, formats scaled amounts the way a cook measures (`2 cup + 2 tbsp` instead of `2 1/8 cup`), and re-resolves all unit ratios along with the schedule. On the shopping list, units from the same measuring system merge onto one line (`1 cup` and `2 tbsp` combine into `1 cup + 2 tbsp`; `1 kg` and `200 g` into `1.2 kg`), ranges add by their bounds (`202-203 g`), and more ingredients work with `gram scale --target`.
- **Whole-section references**: using a section preparation whole (`Roll out the &dough`) now estimates its mass from the producing section instead of warning that the mass is unknown.
- **Consistent timers and 12-hour clocks**: step timers format consistently with non-breaking spaces (`1 h 30 min`, `45 min – 1 h`) across views, `gram cook`, and the run sheet. `gram plan`, the scheduler, and the editor's serving time now accept 12-hour input (`8am-10pm`, `1:00 pm`), with a new `clock` setting (`auto`, `12h`, `24h`) following the reader's region by default.
- **Editor, formatter, CLI, and Playground**:
  - Editors add quick fixes for adjacent quantities (`{1 cup 5 tbsp}` → `{1 cup + 5 tbsp}`), `oz` after a volume (`fl oz`), and percentage sums; hover previews for sums and natural timers; time-only completion inside `~{…}`; and highlighting/inlay hints for ratios and target states.
  - `gram format` spaces `+`/`-` in ingredient sums and trims trailing zeros (`{1.50 kg}` → `{1.5 kg}`, `{1,50}` → `{1,5}`).
  - `gram cook` shows target states under each step (and reminds you when a timer rings), `gram diff` tracks target states, temperature notes, and formatted amounts, `gram import` writes sums, natural timers, target states, and oven notes directly, and `gram shop --format json` exposes numeric amounts alongside `qty`.
  - The Playground adds a read-only `ingredients.yaml` tab so you can inspect the database behind weight and nutrition calculations.

### Other fixes

- `analyze()` no longer mutates the compiled recipe's warnings array, preventing duplicate warnings on live edits.
- Percentages inside alternatives (`@butter{10% @&flour}|@oil{8% @&flour}`) now resolve per option, percentages targeting step-declared intermediates (`->&dough`) in earlier sections are found properly, and percentages on composite parents raise a clear warning instead of being silently dropped.
- Text-only quantities (`@pepper{to taste}`) are preserved in steps, sections, and shopping lists, and zero-denominator fractions (`@flour{1/0 cup}`) raise a warning instead of being misread.
- Fixed `gram plan --find` handling single-digit hours (`9:00`) and invalid clock times (`25:99`, `24:30`), improved narrow ingredient list wrapping, and switched the VS Code preview CodeLens label to English (`▶ Preview`) (yeah, this one is quite specific !).
