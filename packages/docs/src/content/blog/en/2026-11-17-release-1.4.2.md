`v1.4.2` is a small maintenance patch bundling a few fixes spotted while working on upcoming features.

### Bug fixes and improvements

- **Mixed units & shopping lists**: shopping lists now merge different spellings of the same unit (`1 cup` and `2 cups`, `1 c.à.s` and `2 tbsp`) onto a single line (making `gram scale --target` work again for those ingredients), HTML views show every unit used in a section (`100 g + 1 cup`), `gram shop` keeps ingredients whose quantity cannot be computed, and `gram diff` compares each unit separately without flagging spelling-only changes.
- **Step element spacing**: fixed a missing space between two consecutive elements in a step (such as `@flour{500 g} ~{1h}`) across all views and `gram view`.
- **Finished `gram cook` timers**: timers now ring when their time is up and show as done, ready to be dismissed with any key.
- **Single-line preparations**: an unclosed parenthesis in a preparation note (`@flour{200g}(sifted and`) no longer swallows the rest of the recipe — preparations now stay on one line, and unclosed or multi-line notes remain plain text.
- **Temperature formatting**: temperatures with a space and trailing zeros (`^{160.0 °C}`) now collapse to `^{160°C}` in a single run of the formatter.

For the complete list of changes, check out the [Changelog](/docs/changelog/#142---2026-11-17).
