Welcome to the new **Gram Blog**! 

As the project grows, we want a dedicated space to share our thoughts, updates on the language server, new features in the compiler, and of course, some beautifully formatted recipes.

## Why a blog?

While our documentation is comprehensive for learning *how* to use Gram, the blog will be the place to discover *why* we built certain features, how others are using it in their daily lives, and the roadmap ahead.

### What to expect

1. **Release Notes**: Deep dives into new versions of the compiler and CLI.
2. **Community Spotlight**: Highlighting interesting recipes or tools built by the community.
3. **Tutorials**: Advanced workflows like integrating Gram into your own apps.
