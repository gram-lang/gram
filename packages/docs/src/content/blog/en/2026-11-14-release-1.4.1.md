A first round of polish right after 1.4.0! `v1.4.1` is a quick patch to make quantities read naturally in the kitchen, fix interactive timers, and catch a few silent parsing edge cases.

### Bug fixes and improvements

- **Localized numbers and fractions**: numbers now follow the recipe's language (decimal commas in French like `1,5 kg`, no thousands separators in `gram shop` and `gram scale`), while spoons, cups, and imperial measures render as fractions (`1 3/4 cup`) across views and CLI commands. The `formatFraction` renderer option is also now properly applied.
- **Automatic kg/l conversion for totals and shopping**: totals where you shop or read an overview (shopping list, recipe/section weight, nutrition base, `gram shop`) switch to `kg` or `l` from 1000 g/ml up (`3.9 kg` instead of `3900 g`), while steps, section ingredients, the run sheet, and `gram cook` keep grams for your scale. Ingredients written in `kg` or `mg` no longer repeat their standardized mass next to them.
- **Accurate `gram cook` timers**: countdowns now honor the unit written in the recipe (`~{1h}` counts down 1 hour and `~{30 s}` 30 seconds, instead of treating every timer as minutes).
- **Full section highlighting in HTML**: hovering an intermediate preparation (`&dough`) now highlights the entire section that produces it, rather than just its title.
- **Quantity and unit analysis**: an ingredient whose unit holds a quantity of its own (`@tomatoes{1 tin of 400 g}`) no longer falls back to the database's per-piece weight (previously, 1 tin was counted as 1 tomato, skewing the shopping-list total; its mass is now reported as unknown). Quantities split across two units (`@flour{1 cup 5 tbsp}`) or prefixed with words (`@flour{about 200g}`) now raise an `INVALID_UNIT` warning.
- **Section-header parsing and errors**: a malformed section header now raises a clear error instead of a misleading message or silently losing the section, and a section header on the very last line without a trailing newline is now recognized.

For the complete list of changes, check out the [Changelog](/docs/changelog/#141---2026-11-14).
