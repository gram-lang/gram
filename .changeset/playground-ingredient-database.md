**Playground**: The playground now shows the ingredient database behind its examples, so the grams and the nutrition no longer seem to come from nowhere:
  - a read-only `ingredients.yaml` tab sits beside the recipe files, with a short note on what the database is for and a link to the guide on managing one;
  - the file is in the same format as a project's own database;
  - the warnings about an ingredient missing from the database, or lacking data, offer to open it.
